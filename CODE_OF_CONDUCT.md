# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ASOgenic to be a welcoming, inclusive, and constructive project for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or the AI tools they
use. We commit to participating with respect and to addressing harmful behavior
fairly.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and open to good-faith disagreement.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, behavior, and evidence—not people.
- Give newcomers room to ask questions and learn.
- Protect others' privacy and confidential information.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or
imagery, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and sharing private credentials or
customer data are not acceptable. This applies in issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, and any project-related communication.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or limit
participation when needed to protect the community. Report a concern privately
to the maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub profile rather than
opening a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Security vulnerabilities are handled separately; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
